⚠ 注意道路マップ

ドライバー向けのリアルタイム道路情報共有ツールです。
クレームが発生した道や通行に注意が必要な道を記録し、
担当エリアのドライバーへの注意喚起として活用します。

マーキングの目的
⚠ マーキングは「注意喚起」のためです

一目でわかる私有地・私道・明らかに狭い道路などは登録不要です。
「ヒヤリとした」「見落としそうだった」「細心の注意が必要だと感じた」、そういった瞬間に記録してください。

DRIVER'S GUIDE

⚠ Caution Road Map

A real-time road information sharing tool for drivers.
Record roads where complaints occurred or caution is needed,
as safety alerts for drivers covering the area.

Purpose of Markings
⚠ Markings are for safety awareness only

Roads that are obviously restricted — private property, private roads, or clearly narrow lanes — do not need to be marked.
Please record a location when you felt a sudden sense of danger, nearly missed something, or felt that extra caution was absolutely necessary.

Buttons
🔴
Complaint
Record roads where complaints occurred
🔵
Caution
Record roads needing care (type select)
🗑
Delete
Remove lines one at a time (confirmed)
↩
Undo
Revert last action (confirmed)
🎯
My Location
Jump to current location on map
How to Draw a Road
1
Select a button
Tap 🔴 Complaint or 🔵 Caution
2
Tap the map to add points
Tap along the road to trace it
3
Press ✓ to confirm
Tap the green ✓ button at the bottom right
4
🔵 Select type from dropdown
Multiple types allowed. Can also skip and register without selecting.
🔵 Caution Road Types
Private road
Restricted access for general vehicles
Narrow lane
Too narrow for vehicles to pass each other
Height limit
Road with vehicle height restrictions
Width limit
Road with vehicle width restrictions
Unpaved road
Road without pavement
Construction
Temporary works, caution required
Real-time Sync
Everyone's map syncs automatically.
Any addition or deletion is instantly reflected for everyone viewing the same map.
Notes
Deleted data cannot be recovered
Anyone who can access the map can edit it
